LinkedIn, Indeed, Handshake & Glassdoor

These sites block automated scraping (and their terms forbid it), so Jobly uses two reliable routes instead:

  1. Job-alert emails (instant). Create job alerts on each site, set them to send as soon as jobs are posted, and add an email_alerts source to config.yaml with a Gmail app password. Jobly reads new alerts every 2 minutes and fetches full descriptions when you open a job.
  2. JSearch API. Searches LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and ZipRecruiter postings through Google for Jobs. Add a jsearch source with a free RapidAPI key.
